## Problem 1: NY NOAA

### Load the data and describe it

`ny_noaa` data contain the daily weather observations from 747 New York
weather stations from 1981 through 2010. There are 2,595,176 rows and 7
columns. Each row represents one station-day. The measured variables are
`prcp`, `snow`, `snwd`, and `tmax`/`tmin`. Missingness is noticeable,
especially for max/min temperatures (44% NA) and snow depth (23% NA).
